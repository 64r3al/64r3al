## 01 / THE PRACTICE

<div align="center">

### Image. Motion. Form.

**Video Editing · Visual Effects · 3D / CGI · Motion Design · Cinematography**

</div>

I work where **story, atmosphere, and craft** meet. My practice moves between the rhythm of an edit, the construction of a visual effect, and the detail that makes a 3D image feel tangible.

Self-taught and production-minded, I learn by making, testing, and refining. The goal is not to use the most tools; it is to choose the right process for the image, the feeling, and the final delivery.

| DISCIPLINE | FOCUS |
|---|---|
| **EDIT / MOTION** | Rhythm, pacing, transitions, typography, sound, and visual flow |
| **VFX / COMPOSITING** | Integration, atmosphere, cleanup, lighting continuity, finishing |
| **3D / CGI** | Modeling, materials, environments, procedural workflows, simulation |
| **VISUAL DIRECTION** | Composition, framing, color, mood, and visual cohesion |

> **Build the image. Control the atmosphere. Leave nothing accidental.**

## 03 / SELECTED WORK

### [PRO CAPTION AE](https://github.com/64r3al/Pro-Caption-AE)
**AFTER EFFECTS / MOTION DESIGN / CAPTION WORKFLOW**

An After Effects panel that transcribes audio and creates editable, word-synced animated captions. It includes 66 style presets, 15 motion types, and frame-aware timing to make repetitive caption work more controllable and expressive.

[VIEW PROJECT ↗](https://github.com/64r3al/Pro-Caption-AE) · [LATEST RELEASE ↗](https://github.com/64r3al/Pro-Caption-AE/releases/latest)

### [MAP MUSIC PLAYER](https://github.com/64r3al/Map-Music-Player)
**FIVEM / INTERFACE DESIGN / AUDIO INTERACTION**

An in-game music system with a responsive interface, playback controls, and xsound integration—an experiment in bringing UI design, audio, and game environments together.

[VIEW PROJECT ↗](https://github.com/64r3al/Map-Music-Player)
